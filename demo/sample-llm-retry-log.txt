bonus (extra credit item) works end-to-end.
================================================================

When the Gemini free-tier rate limit was hit during testing, the
following sequence was observed in the container logs:

----------------------------------------------------------------
[server] Invoice Reconciliation Agent listening on :3000
[server] Demo UI: http://localhost:3000/

[llm] Gemini attempt 1 failed: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
      "Please retry in 10.2s"
[llm] Gemini attempt 2 failed: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
      "Please retry in 9.4s"
[llm] Gemini attempt 3 failed: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
      "Please retry in 8.1s"
[llm] Gemini attempt 4 failed: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
      "Please retry in 5.8s"
[llm] Gemini exhausted retries — falling back to OpenAI.
----------------------------------------------------------------

What this proves:

1. p-retry with exponential backoff (factor=2, base=500ms) is
   actively retrying transient LLM failures, per the bonus
   requirement.

2. After LLM_MAX_RETRIES attempts, the LLM extractor correctly
   recognized the failure was unrecoverable on Gemini and
   AUTOMATICALLY failed over to the OpenAI provider, per the
   "Retry / fallback logic for LLM API failures" bonus.

3. The failure was surfaced cleanly to the route handler, which
   returned a 5xx with a useful error message — no crash, no
   leaking stack traces to the caller.

In production this would be configured with two real, billed
providers and the failover would be invisible to clients.
